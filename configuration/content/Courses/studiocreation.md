## Navigate to Your Course

- Open **Studio** and head to **Courses**.  
- Select the course you want to edit (one already exists in this demo).

---

## Understand the edX Course Structure

- **Section**  
- **Subsection**  
- **Unit** ← where all content lives (lectures, problems, videos, etc.)

---

## Add or Edit an Assessment in a Unit

- Expand the course outline until you reach the target **Unit** (e.g., Module 3 → Assessment).  
- Inside the unit, click **Problems → Multiple Choice → Edit**.  
- Use the built-in template to create a question:

  - Delete **placeholder text**.  
  - Paste your own **stem** and **answer choices**.  
  - Click the **X** beside the correct answer.  
  - Click **Save**.

- The question renders instantly in the **unit preview**.

---

## Publish the Content

- When the unit is complete, click **Publish**.  
- The material becomes live if the **course start date** is today or earlier.  
- If the start date is in the future, learners won’t see the unit until that date.

---

## Next Steps: Mapping Roles and Skills

- After publishing, you can link this course (and its assessments) to **Roles and Skills** to map competencies—covered in the following step of the workflow.

You’ve authored and published a multiple-choice assessment in Studio and prepared the course for competency mapping.
