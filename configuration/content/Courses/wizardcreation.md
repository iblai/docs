## Launch the Course-Creation Wizard

- Log in as a **Tenant Admin**.  
- Go to **Authoring → Courses**.  
- Click **Add New Course**.  
- Instead of opening Studio directly, a **step-by-step wizard** appears.  
- You can still enter **Studio** later for advanced edits.

---

## Fill In Course Details (Page 1)

- **Name** – the course title  
- **Description** – a brief summary (can match the name for testing)  
- **Duration** – estimated learning time  
- **Course Card Image** – upload an image learners will see in the Catalog  
- **Topics / Tags** – add keywords; press Enter after each one  

- Click **Next** when finished.  
- **Start dates** are auto-set behind the scenes, so you don’t have to manage them now.

---

## Build the Outline & Add Content (Page 2)

- Define the initial structure:

  - **Section** (e.g., Section 1)  
  - **Subsection** (e.g., 1.1)  
  - **Unit** (e.g., 1.1.1)

- Choose your **content type** for the unit:

  - **YouTube URL** – paste a link  
  - **SCORM** – upload a SCORM package directly

- Click **Submit**.  
- **Upload time varies**; SCORM files may take longer than a URL.

---

## Confirm Creation

- After processing, the new course appears in **Analytics → Courses**.  
- From the **Analytics** listing you can:

  - Open **Studio** for deeper edits  
  - Add **credentials or skill points**  
  - **Enroll users**

---

## Verify Learner Access

- Switch to the **Skills** front-end.  
- Open **Discover**.  
- Search for the course name.  
- The **course card** shows the image you uploaded; click **Access Course** to launch it.  
- If **SCORM** was included, it loads inside the **course player**.

---

## Roadmap Note

- This wizard is the **first iteration**.  
- Future updates will add **more options** and simplify choices so authors can launch courses even faster.

---

You’ve now created a complete course shell, uploaded initial content, and confirmed it’s visible to learners—all without leaving the new tenant-admin wizard.
