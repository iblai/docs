# Tenant Creation

## Goal

Create and launch a brand-new tenant (and its first user).

---

## Steps

### Open the Launch Form

- In **Data Manager**, expand **IBL AI Service** app.  
- Click **Launch Tenants**.

---

### Fill Out User Information

- **Username** – choose one that does not already exist.  
- **Email** – enter the user’s email address.  
- **First Name** and **Last Name** – type both.  

- Default roles are set to **Admin** for both **edX role** and **Web role**.  
- Change either role to **None** or **Subscriber** if you need a non-admin user.

---

### Define the Platform

- **Platform Key** – the unique identifier for this tenant.  
- **Platform Name** – what learners will see in their browser tab; spaces are allowed.  
- **Password** – set the initial password for the new user.

---

### Launch the Tenant

- Click **Launch Tenant**.  
- A **success message** confirms the tenant and user were created with the selected roles.

---

### Verify Creation

- Still in **Data Manager**, go to **Core → Platforms**.  
- The newly created tenant should now appear in the list.

---

## Result

A new **tenant** exists, along with its default **admin user**, ready for immediate use.
