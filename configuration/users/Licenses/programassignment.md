# Program Assignment

## Review the Program License

A **Program License** has already been created with:

- **Name** (e.g., “Content Licensing Demo”)  
- **10 seats available**  
- **Active status**  
- Assigned to the **ax Alpha tenant**  
- Includes specific course content

---

## Tenant Admin: Locate the License

- Log in as the **ax Alpha tenant admin**.  
- Open **Analytics → Licensing → Content**.  
- Under **Content Licenses**, locate the license you just reviewed.  
- At this point, no users are yet assigned.

---

## Assign the License to a User or Group

- Click the **license name** to open its details.  
- Choose **Assign**.  
- Select either:

  - **Individual User** – search and pick a single account  
  - **Group** – assign seats to an existing group (all members get access)

- From the **License dropdown**, pick **Licensing Demo** (or the license’s display name).  
- Click **Assign**, then **Close**.

---

## Confirm the Assignment

- Back in **Content Licenses**, click the **license name** again.  
- The **user or group** you chose now appears in the assignment list.

---

## Verify as the Assigned User

- Log out, then log in as the **user who received the license**.  
- Navigate to **Discover**.  
- Search for the licensed content title (e.g., **Licensing Demo**).  
- The **course or program** is visible and ready to launch.

---

## Quick Recap

- **Tenant admins** see unassigned content licenses in **Analytics → Licensing → Content**.  
- Licenses can be distributed to **individuals or groups** from the license detail view.  
- **Assigned users** find the newly unlocked content in **Discover** immediately after assignment.  

With these steps, you’ve successfully allocated a program license and ensured the learner can access the content.
