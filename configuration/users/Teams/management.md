## Accessing the Team-Manager Dashboard

- Sign in with a **Team Manager** role.  
- You land on the **Reports** section by default.

---

## Reports for Individual Learners

### Filter Options

- Set a **date range**.  
- Search by **pathway**, **learner name**, or **enrollment status**.  
- Click **Apply Filters** to update the list.  
- Remove filters to view every learner you manage.

### Download

- Click **CSV** to export the filtered (or full) learner list.

---

## Group Reports

- Shows all teams you manage (e.g., five groups in this demo).  
- Use the same **filter bar** to narrow results.  
- Export a **CSV** for group-level data.

---

## Notifications  
*(Covered in a separate demo, but key points included here)*

- Send messages to specific **learners**, one or more **teams**, or an **individual**.  
- Choose delivery channel:

  - **Email**  
  - **Events** (in-platform)  
  - **Others** as enabled

- Optional: **schedule** a send date.  
- Type your **message** and **preview** it before sending.

---

## Assignments

- You can assign any of the following to learners or entire teams:

  - **Courses**  
  - **Programs**  
  - **Pathways**  
  - **Skills**  
  - **Credentials**

### How to assign:

- Select the **item type** (e.g., Course).  
- Choose the specific **course**, **program**, etc.  
- Search for a **learner** or select a **team**.  
- **Confirm** to add the assignment.  
- All **active assignments** appear in the list below.

---

## Downloads

- Access the same report types tenant admins see, but scoped only to the **learners and teams** you manage.  
- Use these files for **deeper analysis** or **record-keeping**.

---

## Key Takeaways

- **Reports** give learner-level insights with flexible filtering and CSV export.  
- **Group Reports** mirror that functionality for entire teams.  
- **Notifications** let you reach learners directly from Analytics.  
- **Assignments** enable quick enrollment in courses, programs, and more.  
- **Downloads** provide segmented analytics identical to tenant-admin reports—only limited to your own teams.  

With these tools, a **Team Manager** can monitor progress, communicate with learners, and assign learning paths—all without tenant-admin privileges.
