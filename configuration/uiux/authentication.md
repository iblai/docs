## Purpose

Customize what appears on your **login page** for the **Agent**, **Analytics**, and **Skills** platforms.  
*(Example below uses Agent.)*

---

## Steps

#### Open Tenant Settings

- Click your **profile icon** (top-right).
- Select your **tenant name** from the dropdown.

#### Go to Advanced

- Open **Advanced → Authentication Interface**.

#### Edit Login Content

- **Title:** e.g., *Ashland’s agents*  
- **Description:** e.g., *agent AI for education*  
- **Display Logo:** upload an image file or paste an image URL  
- **Legal Links:** add URLs for *Privacy Policy* and *Terms of Service*  

**Carousel Images:**  
- Add one or more display images (upload or URL).  
- *(Optional)* Add **alt text** for accessibility (e.g., *agent chat*).

#### Save

- Click **Save**.

#### Preview

- Log out to view your updated login page with:
  - Title  
  - Description  
  - Logo  
  - Legal links  
  - Carousel images  

---

## Result

Your customized **login page** is applied across the selected platform, reflecting the **title, description, logo, links, and images** you configured.
