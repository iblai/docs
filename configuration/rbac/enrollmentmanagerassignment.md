# Enrollment Manager Assignment

<iframe width="560" height="315" src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/7P49vajFL70" title="YouTube video player" frameborder="0" allowfullscreen></iframe>

## Purpose

Give a user the ability to invite and enroll people into the platform, courses, and programs—without granting full tenant admin or course-editing access.

---

## How to Assign the Enrollment Manager Role

### Open Management

1. Go to **Tenant Settings → Management**.

### Assign the Policy

2. In **User Management / Policies**, select **Enrollment Manager**.  
3. Apply this policy to the user.  
4. Click **Save**.

Assigning Enrollment Manager globally is the quickest way to grant invitation permissions.

---

## What Enrollment Managers Can Do

After assignment, the user can:

- Invite users to:
  - The platform  
  - Courses  
  - Programs  
- Use **single-email invites** or **CSV uploads** (templates available).  
- View invite status:
  - Sent  
  - Pending  
  - Accepted  
- Manage Teams:
  - View teams  
  - Create teams they own  
  - Add users to teams they created or to existing teams  

---

## What Enrollment Managers Cannot Do

- Edit or modify course content  
- Change tenant settings  
- Perform broader administrative actions outside invitations and team assignment  

---

## Enrollment Manager View (What It Looks Like)

- Access to **Teams** tab:
  - See team membership  
  - Add members to teams  
- **Invite** button available across:
  - Users  
  - Courses  
  - Programs  
- Invitation flow mirrors tenant admin UX, but is limited to enrollment tasks only

---

## Typical Workflow

1. Log in as an **Enrollment Manager**.  
2. Go to **Teams → Invite**.  
3. Send invitations via email or CSV.  
4. Track **pending** and **accepted** invites.  
5. Once users register, add them to the appropriate team (new or existing).

---

## Result

Enrollment Managers can efficiently onboard learners and organize them into teams—without access to content editing or tenant-wide configuration—keeping responsibilities clean and secure.
