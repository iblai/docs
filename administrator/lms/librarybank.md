## Purpose

Create and manage questions inside a Library (Problem Bank) in Studio, including adding images to both questions and answer choices.

---

## Add a New Question

1. Open your Library in Studio.
2. Click **Problem**.
3. Choose the problem type (e.g., **Single Select**).

---

## Add an Image to the Question

1. In the question editor, click the **image icon**.
2. Select **Upload New Image**.
3. Choose your image file.
4. Adjust:
   - Dimensions (if needed)
   - Alt text (for accessibility)

You may also mark the image as decorative.

5. Click **Save**.

The image will now appear inside the question prompt.

---

## Add an Image to an Answer Choice

1. Scroll down to the **Answer** section.
2. Click the **image icon** inside the answer editor.
3. Upload the desired image.
4. Click **Save** for the answer choice.

**Note:** Uploaded images are added to your image library and can be reused in other questions.

---

## Preview the Question

1. Click **Preview**.
2. Confirm:
   - The image appears in the question.
   - The image appears in the answer choice (if added).

---

## Result

Your problem bank question now includes images in the prompt and/or answer choices, fully previewable and reusable across your library.
