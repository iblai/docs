# Disclaimers

## Description

**Disclaimers** let you present important information to learners **before they start chatting with an agent**.  

You can require an explicit **User Agreement** (blocking modal that must be accepted to proceed) and/or display a **non-blocking Advisory** (informational notice). Copy is fully editable (**Markdown supported**), allowing administrators to align the language with **institutional policies**.

---

## Target Audience

**Superadministrator · Administrator**

---

## Features

#### Two Disclaimer Types

- **User Agreement** – Blocks chat until the learner clicks **Accept**.  
- **Advisory** – Non-blocking banner/modal (e.g., *“AI can make mistakes.”*)

#### Pre-Chat Enforcement
When a **User Agreement** is enabled, the agent will not allow chatting until it’s accepted.

#### Markdown Support
Write disclaimers in **Markdown** for headings, links, and emphasis.

#### Acceptance Confirmation
After acceptance, a confirmation message appears in the chat.  
Any message a learner typed before accepting is queued and then answered automatically once accepted.

#### Editable at Any Time
Use **Edit** to update the text; defaults are provided for quick setup.

---

## How to Use (step by step)

#### Open Disclaimers

- In the agent header, open the dropdown → **Settings → Disclaimers**

#### Add Content

- Choose the disclaimer type(s) you want to show:
  - **User Agreement** (blocking)
  - **Advisory** (non-blocking)
- Click **Edit** and enter your copy (Markdown supported)

**Example (User Agreement):**

- **Title:** “Course Agent User Agreement”  
- **Body (Markdown):**  
  > “By continuing, you acknowledge that the agent may make mistakes and that you will verify important information with official course materials or staff.”

#### Save & Enable

- Click **Save** to apply changes  
- Ensure the **User Agreement** and/or **Advisory** toggles are **On**

#### Test the Flow

- Start a new chat with the agent  
- Verify behavior:  
  - With **User Agreement** enabled, a modal appears and must be accepted to proceed  
  - You cannot click out of it  
  - On **Accept**, a confirmation appears and any message typed pre-acceptance is submitted and answered  
  - With **Advisory** enabled, the notice appears but does not block chat

#### Iterate as Needed

- Revisit **Disclaimers** to refine wording, add links to policies, or switch between **Advisory** and **User Agreement** depending on the use case

## Pedagogical Use Cases

#### Academic Integrity & Policy Acknowledgment
Require acceptance of **honor-code reminders** or **collaboration rules** before users use the agent.

#### Scope & Limitations
Present an **Advisory** that clarifies the agent’s boundaries (e.g., *“No medical/financial advice; verify critical info.”*)

#### Accessibility & Support
Link to **disability services**, **office hours**, or **help desk resources** in a brief pre-chat notice.

#### Sensitive Topics
Use a blocking **User Agreement** to ensure users acknowledge **content warnings** or **research-ethics guidance**.

---

By configuring **Disclaimers**, administrators keep Agentic OS use **safe, transparent, and aligned** with institutional expectations—while preserving a smooth chat experience once learners accept.
