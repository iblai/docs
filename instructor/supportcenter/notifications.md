# Notifications

<iframe width="560" height="315" src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/eD5d9prlu-c" title="YouTube video player" frameborder="0" allowfullscreen></iframe>

## Description

**Notifications** let instructors and tenant admins send **custom messages** to learners and manage all **automated alert templates** in mentorAI. 

You can compose **ad-hoc announcements** (sent by push and email), schedule them, choose recipients, and control which **system-triggered emails** are active—editing their wording to match your tone, voice, and institutional branding.


## Target Audience

**Instructor · Administrator**


## Features

#### Bell Inbox & Push/Email Sync
View notifications you’ve received via the **bell icon** in the header; many alerts are also delivered by **email**.

#### Custom (Ad-Hoc) Messages
Write your own **subject** and **body**, send immediately or schedule for later, and target specific learners or cohorts.

#### Scheduling
Send **now** or choose a **future date/time** (e.g., next week’s announcement).

#### Recipient Selection
Select **one or multiple learners** before sending.

#### Alert Templates (System-Triggered)
Enable or disable templates (e.g., **Platform Invitation**, **Learner Progress – Weekly**) and edit their copy to fit your tone and policies.

#### Per-Template Toggles
Turn individual templates **on or off**. When active, they fire automatically based on their associated trigger event.


## How to Use (step by step)

#### Open the Notifications Inbox
- Click the **bell icon** in the header to review recent notifications (push/email).

#### Create a Custom Message
1. Click **View All → New Notification**.  
2. Fill **Preview (Subject)** and **Content (Body)**, e.g.:

   > “Hello—checking in on your mentorAI experience. Is there anything we can do to assist or improve your time on the platform?”

3. **Recipients:** Select one or multiple learners.  
4. **Timing:** Choose **Send Now** or **Schedule** for later.  
5. Click **Send** — the message is delivered to learners’ **inboxes** (and **email**, if configured).

#### Manage Alert Templates
1. Open the **Alerts** tab.  
2. Browse built-in templates (e.g., **Platform Invitation**, **Learner Progress – Weekly**).  
3. Toggle templates **On/Off** to control delivery.  
4. Click **Edit** to customize:
   - Wording and tone  
   - Branding elements  
   - Links or policy notes  
5. Click **Save** — active templates send automatically when their triggers occur.

#### Verify & Iterate
- Check delivery via the **bell inbox** and email.  
- Adjust **wording, recipients, schedules**, or **template toggles** as needed.


## Pedagogical Use Cases

#### Engagement Nudges
Schedule **weekly reminders or study check-ins** for specific cohorts; pair with the **Learner Progress** template for consistency.

#### Onboarding & Access
Keep **Platform Invitation** active to automate invites and customize its tone to match your institution’s branding.

#### Targeted Support
Send **ad-hoc guidance** to learners flagged by analytics (e.g., low activity) with helpful resources and office-hour links.

#### Policy & Deadline Reminders
Edit templates to include **exam schedules**, **academic integrity notes**, or **LMS submission deadlines**.

#### A/B Messaging Improvements
Experiment with different **subject lines or message styles** in custom notifications; track outcomes in analytics.


***With Notifications, you control both one-off announcements and automated alerts—so learners get the right message, at the right time, in the right voice.***

# Proactive Learning Recommendations

<iframe width="560" height="315" src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/YLpfRmpVEz4" title="YouTube video player" frameborder="0" allowfullscreen></iframe>

## Description

Proactive Learning Recommendations is a notifications enhancement that automatically sends mentor-generated study suggestions to learners on a schedule you control. Enable the template once, choose which mentors power the recommendations, add an optional per-mentor prompt to steer the guidance, and target who receives the emails and how often they’re sent.


## Target Audience

Instructor · Administrator


## Features

#### Tenant-Wide Template, Mentor-Scoped Content  
Toggle one template On for your tenant, then pick the specific mentors that will supply recommendations.

#### Per-Mentor Prompting  
Add a short prompt per mentor (e.g., “This is a career path planner”) to shape the recommendations learners receive.

#### Recipient Targeting  
Send to All Learners or Active Learners (logged in within the last 30 days).

#### Flexible Scheduling  
Set delivery to Daily, Weekly, Monthly, or a Custom cadence; choose the time and time zone.

#### Automated Delivery  
Once active and saved, messages are sent on your schedule with recommendations based on the selected mentors and prompts.


## How to Use (step by step)

#### Open Notifications → Alerts  
1. Go to Notifications and open the Alerts tab.  
2. Find Proactive Learning Recommendations.

#### Enable the Template  
- Toggle On to activate across the tenant.

#### Choose Mentors & Add Prompts  
- Click Select Mentors and add one or more (e.g., AI Career Path Planner, Assessment Creator).
- For each mentor, click Edit Prompt and enter guidance that should inform recommendations.  
- Save Prompt for each mentor you configure.

#### Select Recipients  
- Pick All Learners or Active Learners (last 30 days).

#### Set Frequency & Timing  
1. Choose Daily / Weekly / Monthly or Custom interval.  
2. Set send time and time zone.

#### Activate & Save  
1. Confirm the template is Active and click Save.  
2. The system will start sending on the schedule you defined.


## Pedagogical Use Cases

#### Momentum & Study Habits  
Send weekly nudges with reading targets, practice problems, or reflection prompts aligned to the course week.

#### Career-Aligned Guidance  
Use a mentor like AI Career Path Planner with a custom prompt to deliver role-specific resource suggestions.

#### Assessment Readiness  
Pair with an Assessment Creator mentor to push practice items or rubric tips before deadlines.

#### Re-Engagement  
Target Active Learners for steady cadence, or switch to All Learners before exams to reach everyone.


***With Proactive Learning Recommendations, you can set it once and deliver timely, mentor-informed guidance—keeping learners on track with the right suggestions at the right time.***
