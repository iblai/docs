# Team Access
You can grant different types of access to a team under the `Management -> Teams`, by clicking the share icon next to the team.

## Access Types
- **Read**: Allows user/group to view the team and its members
- **Edit**: Allows user/group to view the edit the team and its membership
- **View Analytics**: Allows user/group to view analytics about the team
- **Notification Manager**: Allows user/group to manage alert settings for the tenant and send notifications to the team
