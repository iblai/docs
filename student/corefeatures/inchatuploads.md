## Description

The In‑Chat Uploads feature lets you send assignments — essays, lab worksheets, slide decks, problem sets, and more — directly to Agentic OS inside the chat. The agent analyzes the file, provides a grade plus detailed feedback, and (when configured) forwards the score to your instructor’s gradebook. No extra portals or email attachments needed.

---

## Target Audience

**Student**

---

## Features

#### One‑Click File Upload

Use the **paper‑clip icon** to attach documents, spreadsheets, images, or PDFs right in the chat window.

#### Instant AI Grading & Feedback

Agentic OS evaluates your submission against the instructor’s rubric and returns a **score with actionable comments**.

#### Gradebook Integration (Instructor‑Configured)

When enabled, the score is **automatically recorded** in your course gradebook, saving everyone time.

#### Iterative Review

Re‑upload **revised drafts** to receive updated scores and guidance before final submission.

#### Versatile Assignment Support

Works for **essays**, **coding notebooks**, **lab sheets**, **slide presentations**, and other file types your course accepts.

---

## How to Use (step by step)

#### Open Your Agent Chat

Navigate to the **Agentic OS chat** for your course.

#### Upload Your File

Click the **paper‑clip icon** and select your document (e.g., Finance assignment on evaluating risk and returns).

#### Request a Review

After the upload completes, type a prompt such as:  
> Please provide me with a score for my assignment.

#### Send the Message

Press **Send** (or hit **Enter**).

#### Receive Feedback

Agentic OS analyzes your work and replies with:

- **Score / grade** (rubric‑based)  
- **Strengths and areas for improvement**  
- **Tips for boosting your score** on the next draft

#### Revise & Resubmit (Optional)

Make improvements, upload the **new version**, and ask Agentic OS for an updated evaluation.

---

## Pedagogical Use Cases

#### Rubric Alignment Checks

Confirm your assignment meets each **rubric criterion** before final submission.

#### Citation & Formatting Feedback

Upload essays to get pointers on **citation style** (APA, MLA) and **structural coherence**.

#### Lab Worksheet Verification

Submit completed lab sheets to **validate calculations** and receive suggestions for **clearer explanations**.

#### Coding Notebook Review

Send **Jupyter or Colab notebooks** for code correctness, style guidance, and performance notes.

#### Iterative Draft Improvement

Repeatedly upload drafts to **refine arguments, visuals, or data analysis** based on AI feedback.

---

With **In‑Chat Uploads**, you receive **immediate grading and advice**, helping you spot improvements and keep your learning progress on track — all without leaving the **Agentic OS conversation**.
