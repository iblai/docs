## Description

The Phone Calls feature lets you converse with Agentic OS by voice—hands‑free and in real time. Simply tap the microphone icon to start a voice session and speak your questions. Agentic OS responds aloud, creating a natural, human‑like interaction that supports accessibility, language practice, and on‑the‑go learning.

## Target Audience

**Student**

## Features

#### Human‑Like Voice Interaction

Chat with Agentic OS as if you’re on the phone with a tutor—no typing required.

#### Accessibility Support

Ideal for visually‑impaired learners or anyone who finds reading difficult; listen instead of reading long text.

#### Hands‑Free, On‑The‑Go Learning

Ask multiple questions while commuting or multitasking, making study time more flexible.

#### Language Development & Pronunciation Practice

Hear correct pronunciation and practice speaking in a foreign language with immediate feedback.

#### Lower Cognitive Load

Listening to answers reduces screen fatigue and makes complex content easier to absorb.

---

## How to Use (step by step)

#### Open Your Agent Chat

Navigate to the Agentic OS chat for your course.

#### Start a Voice Session

Click the **microphone icon** (voice‑input button). A **phone‑call–style window** opens, and the agent greets you.

#### Ask Your Question Aloud

Speak naturally—for example:  
> Hi Agentic OS, can you help me practice ordering food in Spanish?

#### Listen to Agentic OS’s Response

The agent replies with spoken guidance and may prompt you to respond or practice.

#### Switch Topics Anytime

Simply ask a new question, such as:  
> Can you help me understand the difference between mitosis and meiosis?

#### Request a Quiz Question

Example:  
> Could you give me a quiz question on World War II
Answer aloud; Agentic OS confirms or corrects you.

#### End the Session

Say something like:  
> That’s all for now, thanks Agentic OS.  
Then close the call window.

## Pedagogical Use Cases

#### Language Practice

Role‑play ordering food, booking travel, or simple conversations to improve pronunciation and fluency.

#### Concept Explanations on the Go

Get quick clarifications while commuting—no need to read lengthy text.

#### Accessibility for Diverse Learners

Support students with visual impairments or reading difficulties by delivering content audibly.

#### Rapid Quiz Drills

Request oral quiz questions to reinforce facts (e.g., history dates, biology terms) and answer verbally for immediate feedback.

#### Reduced Screen Time

Listen to explanations of complex theories, reducing eye strain and cognitive overload during long study sessions.
