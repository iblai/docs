## Description

The **Accessibility menu** lets you tailor the **Agentic OS interface** to your needs—without leaving the chat.

Once enabled by your school’s admin, you can adjust **contrast, text size, alignment, tooltips, and more**. Your choices are **user-specific**, so settings stay with you until you change or reset them.

---

## Target Audience

**Student**

---

## Features

#### Contrast Controls
Increase contrast to improve readability.

#### Text Size
Make on-screen text larger or return it to standard size.

#### Tooltips
Turn on helpful hints for buttons and controls.

#### Text Alignment
Choose **left, center, or right alignment** for easier scanning.

#### Per-User Persistence
Your settings apply to your account and can be reset anytime.

> **Note:** An administrator must enable the Accessibility menu for your tenant before you can use it.

---

## How to Use (step by step)

#### Find the Accessibility Icon
If your admin has enabled the feature, an **Accessibility icon** will appear in the Agentic OS interface.

#### Open the Menu
Click the icon to view available options (**contrast, text size, tooltips, alignment**, and more).

#### Adjust Your Preferences
- **Contrast:** Toggle on/off to increase visibility  
- **Text Size:** Increase or decrease font size  
- **Tooltips:** Enable to see brief explanations on hover  
- **Text Alignment:** Choose left, center, or right to suit your reading preference

#### Review Changes Live
The page updates immediately so you can confirm the adjustments work for you.

#### Reset Anytime
Select **Reset** to restore default settings if you want to start over.

---

## Pedagogical Use Cases

#### Low-Vision Support
Larger text and higher contrast reduce eye strain during long study sessions.

#### Cognitive Load Reduction
Tooltips and alignment options make it easier to understand controls and follow content structure.

#### Mobile & On-the-Go Learning
Bigger text helps when viewing on smaller screens.

#### Inclusive Classroom Access
Students with diverse needs can customize the interface to stay engaged and focused.

---

If you don’t see the **Accessibility icon**, ask your **instructor or admin** to enable the Accessibility Menu for your tenant.
