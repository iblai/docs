# Course: Progress Tab

![The Progress tab headed "Your progress", with a Course completion donut at 0% beside a "0% complete / 100% incomplete" breakdown, a Grades bar showing Current grade 0% against a Passing grade of 70%, and the Grade summary table listing AI Assessment (60%) and Capstone Assessment (40%)](../images/course/course_progress.webp)

## Overview

The Progress tab, headed "Your progress", shows "How much of the course you have completed and how you are doing on graded work."

## Target Audience

**Learner**

## Features

#### Course completion
A donut chart of the share of course content you have completed, with a breakdown beside it: **% complete**, **% incomplete**, and, when part of the course is open only to learners on another track, **% locked**. "Note that some content may not yet be released."

#### Grades
A bar comparing your **Current grade** with the **Passing grade** (for example 70%), with a marker at the pass line. A line beneath states the requirement, for example "A weighted grade of 70% is required to pass in this course." When the course uses letter grades, the grade ranges are listed too, and once you are passing the tab says so ("You're currently passing this course.").

#### Grade summary
A table of the course's assignment types with columns **Assignment type**, **Weight**, **Grade**, and **Weighted grade**, ending in a **Weighted grade summary** row. For example, "AI Assessment" worth 60% and "Capstone Assessment" worth 40%. Your weighted grades add up to your overall grade. A course with no graded work says "This course has no graded assignments."

#### Detailed grades
Your score on each graded subsection, grouped by section. Where a subsection has scored problems, **Show** lists each problem's score (for example 1/2) and **Hide** folds them away again. Subsections you can't access carry a lock icon. Until you have a score, the table reads "You currently have no graded problem scores."

#### Certificate status
When the course awards a certificate, a card explains where you stand: what you still need to qualify, when final grades and certificates become available, or a **Request certificate** or **View certificate** button once you have earned one.

#### Related links
Shortcuts to the [Dates](dates.md) tab ("Upcoming assignments and deadlines") and the [Discussions](discussions.md) tab ("Ask questions and talk with the class").

## How to Use

#### Step 1: Check your completion
Use the donut to see how much is left.

#### Step 2: Check your standing
Compare your current grade with the passing grade, and use the Grade summary to see which assignment types count most.

#### Step 3: Find weak spots
Under **Detailed grades**, click **Show** on a subsection to see which problems cost you points.
