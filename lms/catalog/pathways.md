# Pathways

## Overview

A pathway is a guided sequence of courses and outside resources toward a specific goal. The pathway page lists everything on the pathway, lets you enroll, and tracks your progress. The pathway's name appears in the top bar.

Open a pathway from a **PATHWAY** card on [Discover](discover.md), or click **Pathways** in the left rail to see the pathways you are enrolled in. You must be signed in to view a pathway.

Learners can build their own pathways, too: see [Profile: Pathways](../profile/pathways.md).

## Target Audience

**Learner**

## Features

#### Courses
The pathway's items as cards. Course cards open the [course page](course-about.md); items badged **RESOURCE** are outside links and open in a new browser tab. An empty pathway reads "No courses in this pathway".

#### Sidebar
The pathway's banner image, an **Enroll Now** button (hidden once you are enrolled), and a **Progress** bar.

## How to Use

#### Step 1: Review the pathway
Read through the items to see what the pathway covers.

#### Step 2: Enroll
Click **Enroll Now**. You will see "Enrolled into pathway successfully".

#### Step 3: Work through it
Open each course in turn, and return to the pathway page to check your progress.
