# Analytics: Monetization

## Overview

The Monetization tab reports on paid access: revenue, sales, subscribers, and the paywalls you have set up on agents and courses. It appears when your organization sells access and you have selling permission. Paywalls are configured from **Monetization** in the sidebar footer; see [Organization Settings](../organization-settings/overview.md).

## Target Audience

**Administrator**

## Features

#### Revenue cards
**Total Revenue**, **Total Sales**, **Active Paywalls**, and **Total Subscribers**.

#### Charts
**Subscribers by Status** and **Paywalls by Type**.

#### Subscribers
A table of **User**, **Item**, **Type**, **Plan**, **Amount**, **Subscribed**, and **Status**, filterable by **All**, **Active**, **Trialing**, **Canceled**, or **Past Due**.

#### Configured Paywalls
Each paywall with its prices and whether it is **Active** or **Disabled**, filterable by **Agent** or **Course**.

## How to Use

#### Step 1: Check revenue
Read the cards for totals.

#### Step 2: Follow up on subscribers
Filter **Subscribers** to **Past Due** to find payments that need attention.
