# Profile: Activity

## Overview

Activity is the first tab of your learner profile. It summarizes what you have done on the LMS: counts of your skills, credentials, and enrollments, the time you have spent this week, and your standing on the skill leaderboard.

Your learner profile lives at `/profile` and has seven tabs: **Activity**, [Skills](skills.md), [Credentials](credentials.md), [Pathways](pathways.md), [Programs](programs.md), [Courses](courses.md), and [Public Profile](public-profile.md). The Skills and Credentials tabs can also be opened as dialogs from the left rail.

## Target Audience

**Learner**

## Features

#### Activity Overview
Tiles counting your **Skills**, **Credentials**, **Courses**, **Programs**, **Pathways**, and **Hours** spent learning. A tile with a value of zero is hidden.

#### Time Spent
A bar chart of the time you spent learning on each of the last seven days, labelled with the day and the time (for example "45m" or "7h 20m"). Hover a bar for details; days with nothing read "No activity".

#### Profile Information
Cards for **Last Accessed**, **Joined**, **Total Time Spent**, and **Top Content**, the course you have spent most time on, with a link to it.

#### Skill Leaderboard
A chart placing you by **Skill Points**, with your level (**Beginner**, **Novice**, **Intermediate**, **Advanced**, or **Expert**), your points, and a percentile. It appears when both your organization and your own settings show the leaderboard.

## How to Use

#### Step 1: Review your week
Check **Time Spent** to see how consistently you are learning.

#### Step 2: Jump back in
Click **Top Content** to return to the course you have invested most in.
