## Description

An agent that guides users—especially those nearing graduation or seeking internships/entry-level roles—to craft a clear, confident professional narrative: who you are, what you’ve done, what motivates you, and what you hope to accomplish. It collects your answers through a friendly, supportive Q&A and produces drafts suited for interviews, resumes, or LinkedIn.

---

## Typical Flow

1. **Start the session**
    - Say: “I need help crafting my professional narrative.”
    - The agent introduces itself and explains it will ask a few short questions to shape your story.

2. **Answer the guided questions**
    - Shares a sequenced set of prompts to learn: who you are, what you’ve done so far, short- and long-term goals, motivations, and skills.
    - Tone is intentionally encouraging and stress-reducing.

3. **Get your first draft**
    - Produces a baseline narrative (about 3–4 paragraphs)—long enough to tell your story, not so long it’s hard to edit.

4. **Refine for purpose**
    - Offers to tailor the result:
        - Short version (~75–125 words) or even shorter (elevator-pitch length).
        - Long version (~2,000 words): asks for more depth before expanding (e.g., academic focus, on-campus projects, personal perspective, tone preference).

5. **Adjust for audience**
    - Can frame the narrative for specific contexts (e.g., interviews, resumes, LinkedIn), based on your responses.
