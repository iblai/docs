## Description

The **Spanish-Speaking Mentor** enables **real-time, voice-based conversations in Spanish**—ideal for native speakers and learners alike.  
By default, it responds in **Spanish** for immersive practice, but on request, it can briefly **clarify in English** before seamlessly continuing in Spanish.  

This makes it useful both for **Spanish speakers needing support** and for **English speakers learning Spanish**.

---

## Typical Flow

1. **Voice Call (in-app)**  
   - Start a call so the mentor can **listen and respond verbally in real time**

2. **Spanish by default**  
   - Ask questions in either **English or Spanish**—answers are always delivered in **Spanish** for immersion

3. **On-demand clarification**  
   - Request an **English restatement** at any time  
   - The mentor provides a concise English explanation, then continues in Spanish

4. **Flexible use cases**  
   - Perfect for:  
     - **Language practice**  
     - **Multilingual student services**  
     - **Bilingual classroom assistance**

#### Example

**User:**  
> “Describe the average temperature in Colorado during August (3–5 sentences).”

**Mentor:**  
- Provides a clear, **Spanish-language response**

**User:**  
> “Can you restate that in English?”

**Mentor:**  
- Gives a short **English version**, then switches back to **Spanish** for the next prompt
