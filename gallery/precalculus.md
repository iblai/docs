## Description

The **Pre-Calculus mentor** gives learners **concise, textbook-anchored explanations** with direct links to cited chapters—ideal for self-guided study. Powered by the **OpenStax Pre-Calculus 2e** textbook, it can list chapters, break down key topics, and provide step-by-step explanations with example formulas.

---

## Typical Flow

1. **Access the mentor**  
   - Use the link provided by the **ibl.ai** team and open the chat

2. **Confirm the textbook is loaded**  
   - Check the **Data Sets** tab for the **OpenStax Pre-Calculus 2e** textbook  
   - Ensure it has a **numeric token count** and **Visibility** is set to **On**

3. **Start the conversation**  
   - Ask a direct question or use a suggested prompt (e.g.,  
     > “What topics are covered in Pre-Calculus 2e?”    
   - The mentor lists **major chapters** and **core topic areas**, and offers **follow-up prompts**

4. **Dive deeper**  
   - Ask specific questions (e.g.,  
     > “Explain functions and their properties”  
   - Receive **clear explanations** and **example formulas**

5. **Check the sources**  
   - Open the **Retrieved Documents** panel  
   - View every textbook excerpt used along with **relevance scores** and **direct links**
