## Description

The **Italian-Speaking Mentor** helps learners build confidence in Italian through both **text and voice conversations**.  
It supports **pronunciation, sentence structure, and syntax**, while flexibly responding in **Italian or English** depending on the learner’s needs.  

This makes it useful for **beginners starting at a 100-level** as well as more **advanced learners** who want to refine their skills.

---

## Typical Flow

1. **Text or voice chat**  
   - Begin with typed prompts or start a **voice call** directly within the Mentor AI platform  

2. **Pronunciation assistance**  
   - Practice phrases and receive **corrective feedback** on sounds, accents, and phrasing  

3. **English support when needed**  
   - Ask for clarification in **English**—helpful for beginners—while the mentor primarily responds in **Italian** to build immersion  

4. **Flexible depth**  
   - Stay at **introductory conversational practice** or move into **more complex grammar and syntax** as needed  

#### Example

**User:**  
> “How do I pronounce ‘What is your name?’ in Italian?”  

**Mentor:**  
- Provides feedback on the correct sound and asks the learner to repeat  

**User:**  
> “I’m trying to say ‘buongiorno’ but it sounds too American. Can you help?”  

**Mentor:**  
- Adjusts pronunciation guidance and explains how native speakers say it naturally
